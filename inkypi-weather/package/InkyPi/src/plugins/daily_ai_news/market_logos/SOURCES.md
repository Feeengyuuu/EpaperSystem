# Market tile logos

Small marks that identify each index on the 整点新闻 market strip. Each is cropped to the
mark, scaled to tile size and snapped to one pure panel ink plus white (drawn as the page
colour), so the Spectra panel shows it solid.

| File | Index | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sse.png` | 上证指数 | Shanghai Stock Exchange site icon, sse.com.cn |
| `szse.png` | 深证成指, 创业板指 | Shenzhen Stock Exchange logo mark, szse.cn |
| `sp500.png` | 标普500 | Wikimedia Commons, "Standard and Poor's 500 logo.svg" (public domain) |
| `nasdaq.png` | 纳斯达克 | Wikimedia Commons, "NASDAQ Logo.svg" (public domain) |
| `dowjones.png` | 道琼斯 | Wikimedia Commons, "Dow Jones logo.svg" (public domain) |

The marks are trademarks of their owners and are used only to label their indices.
